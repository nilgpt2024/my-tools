Free Online Klotski Sliding Puzzle

Klotski is a classic sliding block puzzle. The goal is simple: move the numbered tiles around the board until they are in the correct order, with the empty space in the bottom-right corner.

Our free online version runs directly in your browser. Choose between a quick 3x3 puzzle or the classic 4x4 "15 puzzle" challenge. Every game is solvable because the starting position is created by shuffling the solved board with valid moves.

Track your moves and time, beat your personal best and enjoy a smooth experience on desktop or mobile. No signup, no ads and no data is sent to any server.

How to Play

  • Select 3x3 or 4x4 mode to start.
  • Click any tile that is next to the empty space to slide it into the gap.
  • Rearrange the tiles so the numbers are in order from 1 to N.
  • The empty space should end in the bottom-right corner.
  • Try to solve the puzzle in as few moves and as little time as possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Klotski?

Klotski is a traditional sliding block puzzle. The aim is to move tiles into the correct order by sliding them into an empty space.

Is this Klotski game free?

Yes. The game is completely free to play in your browser with no signup required.

Is every puzzle solvable?

Yes. We create each starting position by making random valid moves from the solved state, so every puzzle is guaranteed to have a solution.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game is fully responsive and works on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Does the game save my best score?

Your best time for the current session is saved in your browser's local storage. It is not sent to any server.
