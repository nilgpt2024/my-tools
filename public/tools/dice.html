How the Online Dice Roller Works

Our dice roller uses the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues) to generate cryptographically secure random numbers. This is the same technology used by banks and security systems, ensuring each roll is truly random and unbiased.

Unlike physical dice that may have manufacturing defects, our virtual dice are perfectly balanced. The 3D animation adds a fun visual element while the underlying math guarantees fairness.

Dice Probability Guide

Understanding dice probability helps in games and decision-making:

  • Single die: Each number (1-6) has a 16.67% chance of appearing
  • Two dice: The sum of 7 is most likely (16.67%), while 2 and 12 are least likely (2.78%)
  • Average roll: With one die, the expected value is 3.5
  • Standard deviation: Measures how spread out the results are from the average

Use our roll history feature to track your actual results and compare them with theoretical probabilities.
