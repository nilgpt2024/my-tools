Online Word Count vs Character Count: What Is the Difference?

Word count measures the number of words, while character count measures every letter, number, symbol and space. Many platforms set limits by characters (for example Twitter/X posts or meta descriptions), while essays and articles are usually limited by words. Our tool shows both metrics side by side so you can meet any requirement.
