Pie Chart vs Donut Chart: Which Should You Use?

Both pie charts and donut charts show parts of a whole, but they serve different purposes:

  • Pie chart: Best for showing exact proportions. The filled center makes it easy to compare slice sizes.
  • Donut chart: Modern and clean. The empty center can display a total value or label, making it ideal for dashboards.
  • Nightingale rose: Emphasizes differences between categories with varying radii. Great for cyclical data like monthly sales.

Our tool lets you switch between all three types with one click, so you can find the best visualization for your data.

Pie Chart Best Practices

  • Limit categories: Keep 6 or fewer slices for readability. Group small values into "Other".
  • Start at 12 o'clock: Place the largest slice at the top and arrange others clockwise.
  • Use contrasting colors: Make each slice visually distinct.
  • Label clearly: Show percentages directly on slices or in a legend.
  • Avoid 3D effects: They distort proportions and make comparisons harder.
